Step 4: Proceed to the checkout page.

Step 8: Enter your payment details and click "Submit Secure Payment" to complete your order.

Pro tip: If the discount does not apply automatically through the button link, open a fresh incognito/private browser window and click the button again. Some session cookies from previous Hostinger visits can interfere with coupon applications.
